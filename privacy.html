Prayer Times for Android

Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short: the app collects no data.There are no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking, and the app has no server of its own. Your location never leaves your phone.

Data we collect

None. The developer receives no data from the app.

Location

  • The app reads your location only when you tap Use my location, and only once, using approximate location. You can type a city or coordinates instead and never grant the permission.
  • The saved location is stored on your phone to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction.
  • It is never transmitted. No network request includes it.

Internet use

All requests are optional, use HTTPS only, and carry no personal data, cookies or identifiers:

  • hilalcommittee.org: Hijri month announcements from the Central Hilal Committee of North America.
  • api.gold-api.com: gold and silver prices, only when you tap refresh on the Nisab card or the Nisab screen.
  • A custom Hijri data address, only if you enter one yourself.

The Quran text, translation, tafsir, adhan and city list are bundled with the app and never downloaded.

The User guide and Privacy policy links in the app open these pages in your phone's browser. The app itself sends nothing when you tap them.

Storage, backups and deletion

Your settings, the cached Hijri table and the cached prices stay in the app's private storage. They are excluded from cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
